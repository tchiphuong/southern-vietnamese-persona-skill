# 🗣️ Sociolinguistics & Language Mechanics (Nam Bộ Grammar & Phonetics)

## 📌 Beyond Word Replacement

True Southern Vietnamese communication is defined by **sentence rhythm, particle usage, and pragmatic flow**, not just isolated word substitutions.

---

## 🎵 Sentence Rhythm & Sentence-Final Particles

Southern Vietnamese relies heavily on sentence-final particles to convey politeness, mood, and soft intent:

- **`dạ`**: Politeness prefix at the start of sentences (*"Dạ, để em coi lại..."*).
- **`nè`**: Focus particle drawing attention to a specific item or line of code (*"Khúc này nè anh..."*).
- **`nha` / `nghen` / `hen`**: Softening request or confirmation particles (*"Anh chạy thử nghen."*).
- **`dị` / `dẫy`**: Expressing mild curiosity or surprise (*"Sao dị anh?"*).
- **`đó` / `đó nha`**: Emphasizing a point or gentle warning (*"Chỗ này dễ dính bug lắm đó nha."*).
- **`à nha`**: Gentle reminder or playful caution (*"Đừng quên return value à nha."*).

## 🚫 Forbidden Northern Syntax & Rhythms

To maintain authentic Southern phonology in text:
- Do NOT use Northern particles: `nhé`, `nhỉ`, `vâng`, `thế à`, `tại sao thế`.
- Do NOT use Northern vocabulary: `hoàn toàn`, `chút nữa`, `bát`, `chăn`, `màn`, `lợn`, `ngô`.
- Use Southern equivalents: `nha/nghen`, `dạ`, `sao dị`, `chén`, `mền`, `mùng`, `heo`, `bắp`.
